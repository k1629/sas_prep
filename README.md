# SAS Examination Master Hub (Civil Accounts Branch)
### IA&AD Subordinate Audit/Accounts Service Examination Platform

An interactive web application and installable Progressive Web App (PWA) built specifically for candidates preparing for the **IA&AD SAS Examination (Civil Accounts Branch)**.

---

## 🎯 Key Features
- **Strict Syllabus Alignment**: 100% structured according to `syllabus_complete.pdf` across all 8 Civil Accounts papers.
- **Unabridged Statutory Text**: Complete codal text from C&AG Regulations 2020, DPC Act 1971, Constitution of India, GFR 2017, CPWA Code, Civil Accounts Manual, FRSR, and CCS Rules.
- **AI Mentorship & Crux**: Powered by Google Gemini for instant high-yield exam summaries, codal crux extraction, practice quizzes, and 24/7 AI Tutor chat.
- **Audio TTS Reader**: Listen to rules and statutory procedures hands-free during commute or revision.
- **Personal Notes & Text Edition**: Highlight, edit notes, and track chapter mastery (Unread / In Progress / Mastered).
- **Progress Tracking & Exam Countdown**: Real-time 6-month countdown and syllabus readiness percentage.
- **PWA / Offline Mobile Support**: Install directly on Android/iOS via Chrome/Safari. Fully functional offline.

---

## 📚 Covered Papers (Civil Accounts)
1. **PC-1**: Language Comprehension & Office Communication
2. **PC-2**: Financial Rules & Principles of Govt. Accounts
3. **PC-3**: Public Works Accounts & CPWA Code
4. **PC-4**: Civil Accounts & Financial Administration
5. **PC-9**: Government Accounting & Audit Procedures
6. **PC-11**: Procurement, Contract Management & GFR
7. **PC-13**: Civil Accounts Manual & Accounting Rules
8. **PC-15**: Advanced Civil Accounts & Financial Management
